I praise my Lord through Muhammad 
upon Him be the blessing of the Eternal Lord.
Ô Allah bless my life 
and make my  heart  the homeland of your verses [of The Koran] 
You cleansed me from the sin of associating, 
therefore grant me absolute certainty.
To You only I complain about my condition of being poor, 
enrich me and perpetuate my richness.
I confided with The All-Kind, The Generous, 
and The Giver whose kindness extends to Paradise.
I confided with The Primordial and the Everlasting 
who revealed his noble Book and granted me hospitality.
He brings forward what my heart desires 
until my admittance to paradise.
I address Him this statement and I'm grateful to Him for 
His Prophet, and my haters are helpless.
You made the Holy Koran my property , 
then I implore You to preserve it in my chest , and my life likewise.
Make Your Book my companion wherever I am, 
Ô Allah to whom I bow down.
Your brought forward the flawless copies  to me 
and imparted me with certitude.
You removed my poverty and I don’t ask anyone but You, 
Ô best Enricher who is worshipped with indigence.
You embellished Your Book in my life as you adorned my life to Your Book,
I am perfect and loved.
You dictated me the pronunciation of the Holy Koran , 
and Your allies are victorious through my being.
Reciting the Koran with intonation effaces 
the sins by the grace of the Enricher.
Reading the Koran has driven away the harm 
to others [the enemies] and it’s gone.
Harm and evil shall not come near me in my life 
by the protection of my Exalted Lord.
The holy Koran brings forward all my desires without limit 
and I will never be dazzled by the immense graces.
My earthly life rejoices the Prophet,
may the blessing of him (Allah) who dishonors the playful, be upon him.
The Lord of mankind has given me the Koran as property 
through the intermediary of the Archangel Gabriel, and the Holy Prophet.
The Benefactor has been  generous to me, 
and granted me understanding as He benefited me.
The service of the best of mankind  
brought me all the goodness.
The Imaan, Islam, and the spiritual prefection are leading me 
to the straight path and Satan will never come near me.
Allah the Almighty and his Prophet 
are well pleased with me and I am satisfied.
Through the Ever-Livingness of Allah, 
the Holy Koran and the Prophet, I succeeded in my life.
I entrusted my tongue and my heart to Allah 
as well as my limbs and I attained perfection.
He who's protecting me against harm,
and leads me towards Paradise.
I praise my Lord through Muhammad 
What an Excellent intermediary to my Eternal Lord.
