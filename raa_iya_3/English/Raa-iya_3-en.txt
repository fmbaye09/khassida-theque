Allah has separated through our master Al Faruus (Umar)
the truth from falshood that the pagans manifested.
The day he embraced Islam Gabriel came
with the order of Allah who has power over all.
Informing that the news 
is a happiness for those who inhabit the heavens and they are proud of it.
Through him Islam has become triumphant.
Its enormous bounties are unimaginable.
He had educated everyone to guidance 
and they're rightly guided from his guidance.
He's the torch that enlighten the men of God.
Allah said the Truth is with Umar.
Among his miracle we can name that he sent a message
to a motionless river then it has started to flow again.
As for Ibn Affan (Uthmaan) our master,
his bounties are manifest.
He has gotten testimony and the two lights and then
secrets and particularity from the Prophet.
The Prophet spent a whole night seeking Allah
to bless him what a great esteem.
He was martyred, and married two daughters of the Prophet who was satisfied with him.
By him You have effaced the inner and outer shortcomings.
The Prophet said in his regard that he (Uthman) will intercede
for (seventy thousands) people who were doomed to enter Hell.
The Angels are shy of him (Uthman)
as well as the Prophet what a great honor.
Part of his miracle is when a group of people surrounded his house
For (49 days) out of oppression and he was patient.
The angles prayed for his Janaza when he passed away
in accordance to the information given by the Prophet.
Among his miracles when his blood stained 
on the Holy Book in this there’s a great warning.
As for the cousin of the Prophet (Ali) our master,
the husband of Fatimah, Ali is chosen.
He's courageous, knowledgeable and ennobled love, 
nearness, he's the companion and the neighbor of the Prophet.
He’s the gate of knowledge, father of the two grandchild of the Prophet.
He was the first one to receive guidance.
The Prophet has appointed for him
the same position as Harun has occupied toward Musa.
The proof of his bravery is that he lied down
in the bed of the Prophet when the house was surrounded by enemies.
He’s got the garment of the Prophet
and never was he affected by harm from an impostor.
Every enemy aimed to kill
the Chosen One but to no avail (they couldn’t see him).
Allah sent Gabriel accompanied by an angel
so that they protect him against the evil planners.
When the Chosen one went away, they confronted them
with a handful of sand thrown on their faces, then  they fainted.
The blessings of the Sahaba in the almighty God
cannot be written in pen and ink.
I implored God by the blessing of the Chosen One and his Companions
so that I can be satisfied with what You've chosen for me.
O Allah by the Chosen One, the Eraser of sins, I ask you to forgive me.
I spent most of my life procrastinating, and sinning.
From today on I ask You to no longer follow my carnal soul.
Let alone Chaytan, and keep me away from trials.
Cleanse my heart and fill it up with Marifah
as well as the truth, patience, and nearness to God.
Until I part with the evil deeds
and that I stick to good deeds that enable me good rewards.
I beseech from You prayers and peace
upon the Prophet and to his chosen household.
And also send blessings and peace to him
on my behalf, and all of his Companions.
Bring prayers and peace unto him
that will remove  my sins.
Bring prayers and peace unto him
that enlightens my heart eternally.
Bring prayers and peace unto him
that fills my heart with devine secrets.
And that eternally attach to “Tawhid”,
good actions, courteousy and remembrance.
And that I always honor my promise 
without never returning to that which is evil.
And that I may triumph above the enemies 
until the chosen ones, and devine secrets come forward.
That I may also be happy forever 
and he the privileged servant, whose service will please You.
O Lord send blessings upon the Chosen One and be satisfied with him.
May your satisfaction continue to benefit him.
And grant by the Chosen One and his companions
that I never envolve and anything that causes harm.
