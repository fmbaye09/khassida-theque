By the holiness of your face Ô Allah the Generous and the Protector, 
I ask you to direct rudeness elsewhere other than my family 
I ask You to drive away the plague to others than my people,
O You who always  perpetuated my purity.
By the sanctity of Your holy face, I implore You not to direct any harm towards me. 
However, I do ask You to drive forward all favors.
You removed my shortcomings, 
O You who gathered up all advantages for me.
My community is highly safeguarded against plague by Allah, 
through the formula of (Laa ilāaha ilal Laah)
Allah has wiped out of me Zulm (offense ), 
Therefore I neither offend nor am I offended 
The Ever-Lasting has surprised me with great happiness, 
may his two blessings be upon the Guide and the bearer of good tidings [Muhammad]
