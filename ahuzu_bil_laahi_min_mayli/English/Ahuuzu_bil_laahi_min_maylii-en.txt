I seek protection in Allah from inclining to anything except toward what leads to his satisfaction, 
I also seek His protection from turning away from the obligatory, and the supererogatory acts. 
It is incumbent upon me to be an eternal slave of Allah 
and His glorious Book which whoever denies it will be rejected.
My means to reach Allah, His chosen slave and our Master (Muhammad) 
He is the one whom whoever denies his precedence will be downgraded.
That is the intercessor whose intervention is hoped 
on the day of judgment and I am content with him rather than others.
I made an oath of allegiance to him in my heart 
and I did not see him yet, and I  devotedly work for him.
I submit my being to my Lord forever, 
through the Prophet and the Book (the Koran) that has accomplished my wishes.
My entirety is for Allah, I am his eternal slave 
I never follow the footsteps of a tyran or misguided.
I repent to Allah for every innovation from this day on,
and for every covenant broken out of doubtfulness.
He is the All-Protecting who always preserved my entirety 
and drove away illness and anxiety from me.
You protected from the Devil so that he will  never approach my sides,
my covenant to Allah never breaks.
We seek protection in Allah in secret and in public 
from the evil of Satan whenever he leaps or runs.
I ask You O Protector to drape a veil of fortress that will protect me from harm 
and seize by your hands my affairs.
I complain to You about my situation today 
hoping that You will subjugate for me everyone whose heart is infected by a disease.
My doubts and my worries are removed from my heart.
Therefore perfect my affairs and guard my abode.
Iblis the enemy is always attempting to trick us, 
and you are my Lord and protecting friend, preserve me from shame.
Make my stay (on earth) sweet, grant me 
peace and security from Iblis and keep him away from me forever.
Chase him and all his allies away from me 
O Allah make him unsuccessful in this life and the hereafter.
Cleanse my heart from jealousy, grant me piousness 
O You the best who washed away my dirt that affected my heart.
Ward off all enemies from the Prophet forever 
Subjugate them Ô You best of subjugator.
To You belong all the treasures, open them for me and increase my happiness,
protect me against all objections.
I hope that You repelled all corruption from me.
Accomplish my wishes and unveil for me the hidden secret.
I entrust my  physical body and all that you have chosen for me.
to You, therefore preserve me from a bad ending.
O You who own the affairs of all creation, be for me and always protect me 
Send your blessing upon the one to whom love is an obligation.
Muhammad and his companions and grant them peace,
and make everything from me well approved.
