I praised the noble, the Chosen One, the master of the waves,
and in His praises I found nations upon the waters.
My God has favored me with the One I hoped for,
Through my praises which turned away those who rejected Islam.
My aim is the continuation of gratitude to God through the Prophet.
Peace be upon him, for whom my pens have drunk (their ink).
My ink and my pens turn to my Lord,
Servant of the One whose praise has raised me in dignity.
They called me to praise the Prophet Muhammad.
My exodus comes from those who have confronted me for years.
