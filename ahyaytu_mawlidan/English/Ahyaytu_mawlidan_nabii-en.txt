I vivified the  birth of the Messenger (Muhammad) in the year 1883 
and it brought me beyond my expectations in 1926.
Allah has taught me knowledge by his will, 
then Iblis and  his band fled.
On Thursday Rajab Allah has directed 
to me an everlasting miracle.
Allah the creator of existing beings has accepted 
the price of my transaction.
The All-Pure has cured me of illnesses 
and fulfilled my requirements.
Whenever I write or read (the holy Quran) 
I receive a reward from my Lord and I am assured.
There is no limit between me and my Lord, 
and he drives forward my wishes out of love.
There are no boundaries between me 
and the chosen one (Al-Muntaqa).
Allah is my Lord, my friend, and beloved. 
The Chosen One (Al-Muntaqa ) is my intercessor, my friend, and beloved.
Iblis fled to others than me with his allies 
and my flows are always increasing.
The Benefactor has given me bountiful graces,
and he subdued the haughty ones.
I intend to give thanks to Allah through the Koran, 
and Allah has preserved me from shame.
My miracles are my handwriting 
and he has bestowed on me bigger gifts.
The one revealed the Koran has dictated it to me. 
And the Giver of life has preserved  my life till paradise
He dictated to me, purified my mind, and secured 
my entirety and I thank him with my pen.
My pen mended me to the noble army (Ahlu badr) 
and Allah had brought forward the best of my requirement.
My writings repelled the enemies of Allah 
to others than me, and I am successful.
Allah has lifted what I composed back in 1883,
informing me about that event in the year 1926.
