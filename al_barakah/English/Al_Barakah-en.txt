Praise is due to Allah for my eulogy
ever since he chased away Ibliss.
For Allah owner of the seven heavens and the earth
belong my entirety and he emerged me.
He blessed me in all my  actions.
My Lord who imparted me (Kun Fa Yakun).
I am victorious in my transactions and my Jihad
and that's is evident within the army of Allah (they all witness).
My handwriting circumambulates underneath the throne 
amidst the virtuous ones, and they worship Allah.
Iblis fled without stopping
to anyone else than me, and my praise grows continually.
