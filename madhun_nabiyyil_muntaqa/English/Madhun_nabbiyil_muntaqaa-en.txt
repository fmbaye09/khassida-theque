Praise be to the chosen Prophet, who led us to
the house of the God of mankind, the Giver of gifts.
The Prophet's love for his God shaped him,
and led him to the enlightened city.
Muhammad, may Allah bless him,
a Muslim, his superiority became apparent to us.
Muhammad is unique, surpassing all others.
He surpassed the multitude and the gifts are sufficient.
The degree of the Chosen One, the beloved of the sender
with him, the degrees of the messengers are elevated.
Every prophet and messenger who follows
my creation and path is the one to be followed.
Our Ahmad erased what was erased
and for us, he drove away those who did wrong.
To Ahmad, the chosen one, what was not
and never will be possible.
His beauty captivates the hearts of the blind
and his approach suffices for the lost.
He gave us faith and Islam
and the best of kindness, we will not blame him.
He is pleased with my writing every year
and other commendable services.
He did not hide his praise and love for
his garden, his home, and his family.
