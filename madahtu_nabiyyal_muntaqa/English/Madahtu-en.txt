I praised the Chosen Prophet, the light of our times,
for his merciful face, which has never ceased to be merciful.
Protect me from Satan and harm, O my Lord,
with the intercession of the intercessor who led me to His mercy.
I am satisfied with what remains and with the best of His creation.
In praising him, I surpassed Kahb and Hassan.
I raised to the Chosen One of God for a period.
My ink and my pens, and my scales were balanced.
May God remove the illnesses that afflict me with his glory.
As for my heart, may God remove our sorrows.
Muhammad, the Chosen One, is generous and honorable.
May God's prayers be upon him as long as he is with us.
Love for him has led me to praise the Prophet.
May Allah's peace be upon him, for I have not attained His satisfaction.
My God has protected me from harm through the Prophet.
And the Chosen One, may Allah be pleased with him, has been kind to us.
If I am led to serve the Chosen One by God
that is enough for me, for the rest is hardship and Satan.
I praised the Chosen One, my Lord, who erased
the faults that my soul had committed, and led me to forgiveness.
May God erase the intention of harm towards me with the help of the One
to whom I conveyed what surpassed our neighbors.
He called me to serve the Prophet, out of love for him
With my love, he was merciful and never ceased to be merciful to us
