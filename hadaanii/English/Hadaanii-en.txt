My God guided me to Muhammad,
upon whom be peace and blessings.
My ink and my pens turned to my Lord
with writing that pleases the chosen one, my lord.
Blessed is he who follows the guidance of the best of guides.
May the generous and supportive guide remain with us.
My certainty, my absolut certainty, I love what he loves.
A prophet and messenger who was serious with me in eternity.
The gift of the best of creation, which did not reach me
but reached others, and my heart was filled with patience.
If I die, my hopes will be fulfilled
and will be directed to others who are rebellious.
Take the harm and the evil and all of it
and direct it to others, not to me, O Owner of uniqueness.
To the Chosen One, convey my service to your face
and grant me pure blessings with the happiest.
Be witness that I am a servant of the One who sits on the throne,
and that I am a servant of the chosen one, and may he guide me.
I believe in one God, the best of owners.
So be merciful and forgiving to me, and support me.
I trust in You, O Creator of the worlds,
in Your remembrance and in the Prophet, who is bound by Your covenant.
I turn to my Lord and the Prophet Muhammad.
May peace and blessings be upon him.
