My God the Merciful and the Clement: 
I am asking for your assistance and forgiveness for my big sin.
Accept my repentance and grant my wishes 
by the Grace of the Prophet the Intercessor.
Bless him eternally, 
offer him everlasting peace and greetings.
Be my ally in this world and tomorrow in the hereafter.
Be my rescuer as I am insignificant.
Be kind towards me, bless me 
and pardon me as I  am  the one at fault.
Respond to my call 
and be my destiny as I am negative.
Grant me goodness and grant me piety.
Light my heart and grant me knowledge.
Grant me a safe life and make me righteous.
Make my daily life perfect and cover me with happiness.
Eliminate my vices, lift me up, 
cure my ailments and relieve my anxiety.
Don't humiliate me but protect me from  obscurity.
Protect me from shame because I am clumsy.
I am clumsy, a slave to pleasure 
and sinful but you are Clement my God.
Grant me and everyone who follows the right path 
peace in this life and the hereafter.
Peace and greeting to Mustapha 
and grant Him everlasting blessing.
