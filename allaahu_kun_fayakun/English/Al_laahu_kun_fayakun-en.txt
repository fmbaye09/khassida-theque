I Praise the one who provides me divine secrets.
I hope to obtain from him the contentment found in Sourate (At Teen).
My speech, my silence are for Him , and He inspires me.
He who provides me happiness without no difficulties.
My speech are addressed to Him He honored and enriched me.
His support has made me forget my ordeals.
I implore you by the sanctity of the Glorious name (of Allah) to grant me to be (to exist) for the rever.
O You best of teachers who unveil the hidden secrets.
Protect me from evil forever O Lord!
And fulfill my request through the formula (Kun).
I confided in You, and keep my promise
so as not to go back  to my previous bargaining.
Gush forth overflowing devine water of ghayb
into my heart and increase me beyond my expectations.
O Venerable One, Infinite Grace and the Almighty Eternal.
I repent to you for every deeds I have done mistakenly.
You approved all good things I have been hearing for
without reckoning, blame and you enrich me.
Grant me from today on Kashf that will stay with me forever
And make my stay (on earth) spent on Fard (obligatory recommendations) and Sunnah of the Prophet.
You awakened me, therefore increase my knowledge and my cognizance.
Make my earthly life a never-ending reward.
