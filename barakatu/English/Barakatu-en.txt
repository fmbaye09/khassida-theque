The blessing of Him to whom Existence and Primordiality 
are incumbent upon Him increases my service.
I praise the owner for Dissimilarity and Ever-lastingness 
who secured me without divergency.
The kingdom of the Self-Sustainer 
(Allah) has made my entirety a miracle.
All forms of praise are due to Allah the unique, 
to whom these (six) attributes are exclusively for Him.
To the owner of Majesty, Will 
and life belong all servants.
Everything in creation is a property of Allah who hears all, 
sees all, and speaks, He who helped me.
He is the All-powerful Glory be to him the possessor of knowledge, 
Will, and Life he's the Unique.
Our Lord is All-hearing, All-seeing, 
and All-speaking and he generously granted me abodes.
Never shall approach to the owner of the creation, 
None-existence, the occurrence of having a beginning or annihilation.
The Lord of the creation has no likeness, 
and never shall poverty touch Him, He owns everything in resemblance.
My praise is dedicated to the Ever-Living which is above Plurality, 
Procreation, and Origin and has no equal.
Never shall aversion or disability approach our Lord, 
nor does He slumber.
I praise the One who is free from ignorance, demise, 
and deafness and he removed my anxiety.
Allah to whom blindness, Dumbness never approach, 
has benefited me, Glory be to the Supreme Judge.
Bringing into being (the creation), or refraining from it is not compulsory to Allah, 
He prevented you from the sin of association.
There is no other deity to worship except Him, 
and I am content with Him from asking anyone else.
Our Prophet and Messenger (Muhammad) 
was chosen by Allah.
He is protected by Allah with truthfulness, trustworthiness, 
and by transmitting the message to us. 
Allah who has made it unlawful for them (Messengers) to lie , 
breach their faith or conceal the truth, has approved my service. 
Through Muhammad the seal of prophethood 
who was given preeminence.
