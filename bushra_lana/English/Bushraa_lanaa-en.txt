Happiness has come to us 
during these (four) sacred months.
We have gained pardon and forgiveness, through Rajab, 
and we hope to obtain the same for the three remaining months.
We expect our Creator, 
the All-Compelling, the Unique, to subjugate for us the enemies He is the Powerful and Avenger.
He is the one sought for help whom I beseech his aid 
and I don't retaliate.
He is the Judge whom whatever He desires will happen immediately, 
and whatever He does not will never take place.
The entire creation, their actions 
are all for Allah Glory be to the Primordial.
Whosoever ascribes a son to Allah 
while having faith in the Trinity, the latter is in plain error.
I recommend you repent to Allah ô you who advocate the trinity.
Hasten to Allah before it is too late then you regret it later.
Hasten to your Lord with submission
by following the tradition of the Prophet and his allies.
Allah is Ever-Living and Primordial and He does not resemble 
anything that a mind conceives he's Unique.
Whoever believes in the trinity besides Allah the Only one God,
the latter is ignorant and will suffer the consequences of associating.
You are enslaved by the Devil O you who associate partners beside Allah, 
you are deaf, dumb and blind. 
I certainly praise the Lord free from an associate 
in the land as in the sea and he is the All-Embracing Judge.
I call on him and he never deceives me 
and He filled my heart with wisdom by his command.
He is the one whose support rejoiced me 
and removed doubt and anxiety from my heart. 
I wish people knew for a fact that my Lord is with me 
and he has removed my shackles. 
He is always for me and I dedicate to Him,
my pen, my heart and my whole being.
My praise through the Coran which I recite with intonation 
during my whole lifetime until I depart for the next world.
It is the book in which whoever serves it 
will transcend their peers in knowledge actions and characters.
I will always worship Allah through the Coran 
by serving the intercessor the generous (Mouhammad PBUH).
He is the generous one to whom I except from his service 
that which surpassed what (Harim) offered to (Zouhair).
I expect from this service to be ranked
among the virtuous companions of the prophet the ennobled.
May Allah send prayers and peace upon him 
and his companions, and may He tame the apostate. 
May He bring forward all I desire without my asking 
out his love for He is the quintessence of generosity.
As long as Allah is my Lord, and prophet Mouhammad the Chosen One is my master 
and the sacred months always bring to us joy and contentment.
