The Messenger of Allah is pleased with my birth.
May he remain with us, fulfilling the best of wishes.
May my God protect me from all harm, 
by the grace of Him to whom all time has turned away the paths of idle talk and corruption.
Muhammad, the chosen one, has no equal.
And there will be no one like him, O my Lord.
My desires from my Lord have come to me without harm,
by the intercession of Muhammad, the intercessor of the worlds.
I trusted in the Lord of the Throne, a servant to Him.
And my heart remained content with this restriction.
For the good of the righteous, from my pen comes good tidings.
And for me, it is a special gift that guides the soul.
He called me to revive this month, for it is...
For the good of the world, a circumstance that encompasses all darkness.
Our gifts are pure foreever in front of God.
And from us, erase the call to my footsteps
Our desire is for the truth, and the truth is our religion.
By serving the wise, he guided all those who were guided.
All corruption is directed towards others.
And good things come to us from the best of helpers.
I have abandoned everything that does not belong to God.
And my Lord has abandoned me to the envy of others.
To God, the Chosen One, I directed what was in it.
I contained pure water, the best of sources.
