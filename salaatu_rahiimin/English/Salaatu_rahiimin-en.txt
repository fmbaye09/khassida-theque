The prayer of a merciful one who has always been the most merciful
upon a servant who preserved his soul and body.
I rejoiced that Allah is my Lord and Protector,
and that the One who is worthy of praise has protected me with His kindness.
I raised my hands to the Most Merciful and He extended to me
from Allah what has been hidden from every human being.
I extended my hand to the Owner of the Throne with the Chosen One,
and my Lord extended my reward without measure.
He called me to praise Him with my prayers upon the Prophet.
And my love has adorned my rhymes and measure.
I have attained the satisfaction of my Lord and the satisfaction of my intercessor.
May the prayers of Allah be upon him at all times.
My desire is to be a servant of my Lord, His servant.
May the peace of Allah be upon him, what Allah sometimes does.
I have a book from Him that has been revealed.
It repelled all devils except myself.
When I recite the verses of the book with which
my soul, my life, and my homeland are purified.
May Allah remove from me anything other than His satisfaction, as
He has purified us with it after forgiveness.
He called us to thank Allah, my leader, to the Prophet,
with service that surpassed that of others.
Thousands of prayers with peace without end
upon the servant who preserved my soul and body.
