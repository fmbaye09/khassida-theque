I praise Allah owner of creation 
for having benefited from the Book (Koran) which protects me.
My heart never wanders to anyone except Allah.
To Him, I direct my speech, and my ears.
I never doubt in my heart 
about what Allah has given me.
My Lord’s Book (Koran) has wiped out all doubts 
and to others, he subduedly led the enemies.
Allah has threatened my haters.
He sent them away from me without no attention.
My whole life is gratitude to Allah until I enter Paradise 
trough the Koran which contains unlimited benefits.
I am convinced that the best creation 
has received revelation from the trustworthy (Gabriel) and obeyed.
O Hadeeth of the Prophet you are authentic.
My words are directed at you (the Prophet) with command.
My ink, my pen are all dedicated to you.
I will never go back on my words.
You are my best friend, as well as the Book (Koran).
You have been with me in all circumstances.
My happiness is increasing until Paradise
which is eternally Pure.
My haters fled from me 
from among mankind, without exception.
