I ask Allah the Majestic for forgiveness and I repent
to HIM, apparent and hidden sins.
I ask Him by the Grace of the Face of ALLAH
absolution from all that is forbidden.
I repent to Him of all the little sins
great sins and what comes close to it.
VERY FORGIVING, forgive me all of my first sins
all of my last sins and those in between.
Make me understand science and increase my science
my works, my politeness as well as my understanding.
LORD, reveal to me all the useful sciences
and save my life from damnation.
Reveal to me the secrets (of the saints) as well as the “hidden” knowledge
O Thou the Best who uncovers hidden secrets.
Gather me all that comes from
my writings, as benefits, and purifies my existence.
Bring into my thoughts the greatest, O
 LORD, by the verb "Be", and lift up my pen.
Grant me O GENEROUS, through my verses
many blessings from You as well as the passion of worship.
Bless me O ALLAH, in my life
and make my heart the abode of the verses.
Make me the joy of all repentant
and take away from me both apparent and hidden sins.
