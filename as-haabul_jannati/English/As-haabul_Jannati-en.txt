To You O best of creation may peace be upon you
from your servant whom you preserved from doubts.
May prayers and grace be upon you
as well as your family and Companions from Allah the Peaceful.
You have acquired a position that no one will ever have.
O you the light of everyone who stands up to worship Allah or is fasting.
You are the one who brought us out of the darkness 
into the light of him who gave you the words.
May the All-Beautiful rejoices you.
May He complete all my intentions for you.
You are the Messenger,the Prophet and the Guide.
You guide all saints under your control.
You have earned your desires from Allah the master of creation 
as the same Way as you’ve gained a high position in his kingdom.
The Ever-Lasting granted me favors
by your grace, you’re my master the best of creation.
I intend to express my gratitude to him every year
and every month and my support is the Book (Kuran).
I intend to express my gratitude to Him by your grace with my pen
and other than that. O You the one who possess wisdom.
Here is my pious gift please accept it.
O you Who brings me all my needs from your Lord.
I repent to Allah through this poetry.
And he graced me favors before.
My pious gift last until Jumada Thani (6th month)
and starts from Muharram (1st month) of Muslim calendar.
The All-Protecting made me inherit pious gifts 
that protect against harm, and are sufficient.
I rejoiced him through through my incomparable service
that no one can produce the likeness and is sustainable.
My words rejoices the Prophet.
My service will accompany him until Paradise.
My pure pious gift to the prophet 
are goodness and eternal happiness.
My pen are gifts for the Prophet
until entry of Paradise of Allah.
My service to the Prophet is manifest,
it protects against any harm.
The position of the Prophet is surely high.
It raises up whoever loves him, and overshadows whoever hates him.
I dedicated this qaafiah poetry to the Prophet.
An ever lasting peace that never stop.
The Personality of the Prophet is manifest.
His life is a healing for everyone.
My extent love to the Lord and the Prophet 
has soften things for me and I am always mindful.
I confided with him until Jumadah thani
beginning from Muharram that enables Paradise.
