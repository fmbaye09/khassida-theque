My gratitude is to my Lord, Al Karim, the Generous, 
the Master of graces hoping for blessings from Him that He has never granted before to any creature.
I write in gratitude for the graces the Lord has bestowed upon me, 
hoping for salvation, gratitude and remembrance in return.
I direct my whole being in this month of Mawloud, the Month of the Envoy, 
to the Most Generous, the Exalted One, the Supreme Giver, the Grateful One.
Hoping from the Lord, the Most High that He will be pleased with my works 
and raise them to Him, the Absolute Absolver.
I ask Him to absolve me of all my most venial sins 
and to tame the infidels.
I address Him from my place of exile at sea as a persecuted person, 
but He has never ceased to be Al Jalîl, the Majestic, Al Qahhaar, the Supreme Dominator, Al Jabbaar, the Irresistible.
Lord, after surprising me with immense graces, 
now bring me back to the land and neutralize any opponent who challenges me.
O Lord, grant Your grace and salvation to the Prophet and his noble companions, 
raise me and increase my blessings afterward.
