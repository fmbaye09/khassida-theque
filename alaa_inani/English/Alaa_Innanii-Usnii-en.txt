I certainly praise the Best Benefactor 
and I never complain to the creatures about lack of favors.
Anyone who believes that because of my exile I became submissive or humble 
before anyone other than Allah,The Enricher, and The Boundless, then the latter is such a blind person.
It is to Him that I complain about my weakness, poverty and my lonesomeness 
hoping that soon He will bring me home full of blessings.
When I complained, to him about my weakness, He has immediately fulfilled it.
beyond my expectation for He is the Most Generous.
The infidels who had been beguiled by their blasphemy stepped away from me.
Allah has preserved me from them and chased away all criminals.
I am  thankful to Him hoping to obtain from Him all my desires 
without difficulty and my anxiety was removed.
If certainly jealous victims of their false opinions have treated me badly, 
He who has always surrounded me with benevolence, has never abandoned me.
Whoever cast me into the Ocean out of hatred and disbelief 
then the generosity of Allah has reached me at sea.
Whoever disapproves of me because my devotion to my Master 
and the service I render to the supreme intercessor whom precedence was given.
I will remain the slave of Allah who worship Him wherever I am Both on land and in the sea
and the privileged servant of Him who preserves from that which causes regret.
Whoever seeks to turn people away from me, then the favors are pouring 
from the Most High Supreme Preserver.
Whoever abandons me in the ocean intending to make me feel lonesome.
They act in vain owing to the fact that my Lord has granted me meadows throughout the surface of the Earth.
It is to my Lord through the Chosen One my means to reach to Allah
that I take refuge in my presence and my absenteeism.
May Allah bestows upon him prayers and peace 
to his household, his companions and all Muslims.
My Lord, upon the best of creatures Muhammad 
eternally send the purest salutations and bless him.
And to his household, his companions and allow me to return quickly
back to TOUBA by protecting and educating me.
Lord, I pledge to remain forever the servant of the Prophet 
and the servant of the verses (Quran), instruct me and deepen my knowledge.
I implore You, the Most Generous, to count me among the erudite.
Inspire me knowledge and lift the veil for me.
I turn towards the Opener (Allah) seeking His grace.
I also hope to obtain from Him overflowing bounties.
I am satisfied with The Subduer (Allah) since he has tamed my enemies 
when I felt alone  and confused.
As a token of gratitude to Him for the benefits that he has granted me,
I always seek to please Him by conforming my heart and body to the teachings of the Quran.
It is incumbent upon me to follow the path that is laid out for me 
and to please Him with the compliance with the Quranic verses which are my treasure.
My expression of gratitude and my satisfaction are for The Benefactor 
who brought down a Glorious sacred book (the Quran).
It constitutes the best treasure, the gushing light 
out of which we protect ourselves from shame, enemies and Hellfire.
It is a Glorious Book from a Venerable Glorified 
transmitted through a Noble and Trustworthy (Archangel) to the Noblest and the integrant (Muhammad).
A Noble Book from an Honored and revered Lord 
descended upon a slave who rescues and asks Allah for mercy Muhammad.
May our Master grant him an eternal and extensive blessing 
to his household and his companions and whoever is affiliated to him.
May Allah bless him as long as a thankful servant will say
that I certainly praise  the Best Benefactor.
May Allah greet him on my behalf 
and I never complain to anyone about my lack of favors.
May salvation that will preserve me from evil be upon him 
as well as protecting me against shaytan and blindness.
May Allah grant him peace as long as a  complainer will triumph 
to the best of his requirements a complainer would ever require.
Through his noble  household, his virtuous companions I beseech 
to become integrated into their company filled with favors.
