for the evident truth free from error.

and by its grace we will be protected against torment from both worlds.

We have vanquished through it for all our wishes.
It has become a sustainable treasure for us.
By his grace, Allah will preserve us from the evil of all creature.

that our peers can not find , and increase my love for Allah.
My enemies are repelled through the Quran , and it drives me 
toward the Most-Generous the Giver whether I am farmost or near.
I am disconnected with all enemies, doubt and plague 
through the Quran and I rose up after hardship befallen me.

And by the blessing of him to whom singing his praises had become my habit.
Upon him be the salutations of Allah Exalted He is,
to his household and companions the best group.
