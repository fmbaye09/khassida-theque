He traveled at night in the company of Archangel Gabriel,
on Al Buraq, his Impetuous Mount, towards the Spheres unknown to mortals.
He ascended at night [to Heaven], meeting in playfulness all the Messengers
and Prophets, Most Respected to the Elected.
He received a Gracious Welcome from everyone and everyone enchanted Him,
by magnifying Him with an Honor befitting to a Glorious.
He imputed [from the Night Travel] what He got; a Mystery Proper to Him,
to which no creature can claim, However eminent it may be.
He returned the same night, before dawn,
in the midst of his own, abundantly laden with Impeccable Gifts.
It belongs to Him, Unusual Phenomena of which we cannot discover the wonders,
that, doubtless, neither ink nor pen can describe.
The strain of the Prophet, due to the displacement of the latter,
lamented like the whining of a mother who was deprived of the company of her child by death.
His Servant (the Sheikh) summoned Him to sea to help him before the affliction,
in this He removed sorrow from him.
His two Hands of Generosity and Repression are well known by the enemy and the beloved;
Liberality flowing from the Right Hand.
The Left Hand Repressive Force Protecting Those Seeking His Help
against obvious or hidden danger.
The Servant called the Son of Abdallah, being sick,
and He healed the heart and the body and consolidated the spiritual will.
Water gushes out of the well, when the Chosen One (Al Mukhtar) threw out some of his saliva,
and ends up breaking like a wave.
A stag has reached the Selected (Al Muntakha), addressing to Him;
the iguana spoke to Him like a being endowed with reason.
A giant tree bowed down to the Purest Chosen One (Al Mustafa);
a camel complained to Him of an evil which tortured him.
The one who shows his gratitude [the Sheikh] had recourse to Him in the past
and is as if he has never known malice or disease.
Upon Him, the Prayer of ONE Who put Him above all creatures,
on his family whose generosity far exceeds the rains.
Upon Him, the Peace of the LORD Who has increased his Eminent Authority,
on his Companions who are the lights of those who have removed the darkness.
That they be granted all the time the Approval of the One at all times
Whose Way they've taken, each avoiding retribution.
His Companion fighters encountered difficulty,
when they caught a large number of very haughty libertines by surprise.
He [the Prophet] appeared as a Full Moon on the Day of Badr in the head of the Muslims,
with the intention of raising the Voice of ONE Who Exalts their Will.
Because, Alone among his Companions, a crowd of damned who are never grateful
to [GOD's] Benefits, This Day came to Him, taking Him to task.
The virtuous people were led to Badr by their happiness;
and each, determinated, relied entirely on GOD.
The Companions rushed together, in virtue of their attachment to the Prophet
for the Love of their CREATOR, so as to exclude any accusation.
There were among them the caliphs: the truthful (Abu Bakr), as well as Omar, Othman,
as well as Ali, the one who fascinates the scholars.
For the heart of Othmane was present among them like all combatants;
as for the other three, their physical presence was not occult.
The truthful (Abu Bakr) immediately believed Him at his word by the Truth,
being submissive and free from hesitation.
The loyal discriminator (Faruqul Huda), in this case Omar, has separated himself from doubt;
and the man with the two lights granted himself the benefit of the circumstance without difficulty.
Then the eminent Ali ended up getting a higher rank,
for he never ceased to cross the army of the enemies.
May GOD's approval befall them all the time,
as the Light which drives away darkness spread over them to enlighten them.
They have extraordinary wonders that have brought joy to us
bringing us advantages whose serenity is never disturbed.
Thanks to them, GOD Who is with us, spared us, without pain,
from the fight to His Paradise and this, by Pure Grace.
They have killed the enemies of GOD, since they revealed themselves with their maneuvers
on the Day of Badr, each of them having surrendered to GOD.
When in front of them, they saw the enemies,
He [the Prophet] taught them things which make us always happy.
The Prophet then consulted with his Companions to honor them,
and then uncorked Abu Sufiyan's caravan.
Without their misfortune, they would not have headed
towards Badr, but they are, for their part, blind stricken with dumbness.
If it was not for the happiness of all the companions of the Prophet,
they would not have gone to their Badr, each very determinated.
They marched, some hardly, others fearless, towards Badr,
for the success of the blessed and the misfortune of the losers, this is well known.
GOD, the VERY HIGH, has in this an Intact Secret
that He entrusts to the Sages in His Decrees.
After consulting each other [auxiliaries and emigrants], they came with their advice;
among them, Sahad made remarks which removed any ambiguity.
Then Ibnul Aswad expressed his agreement that the Prophet should lead them [them Ancars],
even up to “Barkul Ghimâd”, testifying to unfailing sincerity.
Our lord Omar the discriminator (Al Faruq) made a statement after these two
on the numerical superiority of enemies over Muslims.
They raced to Badr after their determination
and kindled the fire of far-reaching combat.
They used swords and spears among themselves,
so that the dust rose thickly towards the sky.
The MASTER of the Throne brought a [Heavenly] Army against which
a troop of mutes deaf to His Signs cannot struggle with firm feet.
A Cavalry of Angels armed with Lances routed
in Badr a cohort of deaf mutes.
A thousand (Angels), followed by a thousand and a thousand others, rushed forward
on corpses at that time, and thus, from the GLORIOUS LORD.
When they reached Badr, there was among them
Archangel Gabriel on (Hayzum), [his mount] who is impetuous.
They went against all disbelievers and all proud,
from Heaven, like a heavy and heavy downpour.
Their cloud of dust veiling the mountain valleys, Gabriel shouting with satisfaction "Forward Hayzum";
and the Best Creature threw [its handful of sand].
Thus appeared in his bewilderment Abu Jahl who was disillusioned [informed of the bitter reality]
by a man of the Right Way who immediately amputated his leg and put him to death.
