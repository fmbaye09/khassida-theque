Guide us all on the right path,
make us strong and not deficient (both physically and spiritually).
I address You, O the Near, O the One who answers prayers.
O He by whom the supreme miracle has bound itself to me.
Grant me and my disciples
a straightness leading to grace.
It is You alone that we adore and it is You alone that we implore for help.
You are certainly the One we adore and who helps.
You have enlightened us about You, about Your dimension, O Vigilant
O Thou, with whose grace and towards whom, all clairvoyant walk.
O Holder of the straight path, grant us
a straightness such that our predecessors will envy us.
