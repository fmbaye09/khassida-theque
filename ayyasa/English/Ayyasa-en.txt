Allah had caused Iblis (Shaytan) to give up on me, at Jewol, 
when I besought Him by saying: "O Protecting Friend !"
By the blessings of the angels of Badr, ALLAH had repelled anything 
that entails hardships, before Ndar (St Louis).
In Dakar, ALLAH had repelled 
any harm planned by the whisperer of evil [Satan].
Aboard the ship, the Most Beneficent, had informed me that verily, 
I am the Assistant of the Apostle of Medina.
At Conakry  [Guinea], the Evolver had washed off of me 
that which leads to gracelessness.
At Grand-Bassam [Ivory Coast], Allah had deviated 
anyone from attempting to confront me.
At Dahomey [Benin], the Most Merciful had given me 
overflowing graces that required no supplication.
At Libreville [Gabon], Allah had graced me with something 
that no Wali (Friend of ALLAH) had ever gained access to.
ALLAH had yielded to me, companionship with the angels of Badr, 
who had repelled any possessor of wickedness or hatred.
Allah brings forth to me, wherever I am, the sweet joy yielded 
through the repetition of "Laa Illaaha Illal Laah" (There is no deity, except Allah).
At Mayumba [Gabon], which broke my heart, 
had been cast away, and my endeavor rose.
At Galwa [in Gabon], I have gained access to the true essence of Allah;  
and he has gotten away from everything that leads to destruction.
My mystical lyrics have been brought back 
to life amid the ocean.
The All-Knowing had informed me at the majestic oceans, a form of knowledge He willed, 
and he had gotten away from the female idolaters.
At the drowning oceans, Allah is getting away from the wickedness of the female hypocrites, 
and from those who invite to the consumption of alcohol.
Allah, the Most Beautiful, had made accessible to me, amid the accursed females, 
rejecters of faith, that which preserved me from their temptations.
The graces, which have been led to me, amid the lofty oceans, 
have yielded the divine joy and dazzling lights.
He (Allah) Who hastened me from the light of day, 
amid the dark places and those who wronged their souls;
My blessedness in the sight of  Allah is manifest 
when I was amid those given to entertainment.
Nothing other than the pleasure of ALLAH, the Everlasting, came near me, 
in the midst of those given to the consumption of alcohol.
Allah had purified me amidst of the people of the Book, 
has protected me from damnation and from anything that results in admonition.
He, who had led certainty of knowledge to me, has honored me, 
in the midst of those who are engaged in idol worship.
I have communicated with Him, through "O Protecting friend!  O the Helper!  
Verily You have shielded me from Iblis at Jewol."
