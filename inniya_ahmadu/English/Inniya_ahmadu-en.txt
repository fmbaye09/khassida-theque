I pay homage to my Guarantor Educator,
praying on my imam-tutor (guide).
Our Prophet, our most beloved messenger;
Our rescue, our responding interceptor.
His savior Mahi Muhammad
in whom we place our hope here and beyond.
On his family, his Companions and those who follow them
Afterwards it is You who are sought, O Assistant.
Grant me your Mercy and forgive both my parents
and to all people, accept our step.
Facilitate for us all that will be useful to us.
Protect us from all that hurts us.
Graciously facilitate all that is difficult
and puts all people under my dominion.
O Benevolent, O Vigilant, O Keeper of Accounts.
O the Just, O the Majestic, O the Granter.
You are my ally here and beyond.
You are my hope forever.
My prayers and worship are all for You
(for) my life and my death are in Your hands.
You have my soul and my word, O the Majestic.
Yours is the relief of my anguish, O All-Knower.
You are the climate that softens my task, O the Wise.
And You are the destination of my plant, O Omniscient.
Prepare this world for me to be the best.
Be forever my ally and my helper.
You have the heavens and the earths
and everything that comes out of it.
By Your royalty and praise together,
You give life or death out of nothingness.
By Your greatness everything becomes docile.
O Lord whose strength dominates all living.
O the holder of Majesty and greatness.
O You who give luck and fortune.
We are poor, grant us Your mercy forever,
we are in front of Your door which is the path of the happy people.
O Lord give us all the best substance,
chosen for us by Your grace the best of our desires.
Add me knowledge, piety and politeness
until I became wonderful in front of people.
Make my heart sweet to worship
and give me dominion over all the oppressed.
It's You who made me loved by people
until they think I'm the chosen one.
But I'm convinced that I have nothing else
than the mantle of Your grace.
Be forever by my side and never let
someone else to take care of my situation.
I beg your pardon. O the Clement and entrust me
to You against Satan whom I flee, O Lord.
O Lord, make my affair good
and the affair of my companions and that of the Muslims.
Lord make me follow the useful way.
And be my companion, O best companion.
I am confident, O the Preserver,
I will never associate anyone with You.
Grant me protection, refuge
and the discretion that does not wash away trial and evil.
And that Your protection, that of the Koran
and that of the Messenger of God remains in all times.
O our Lord! O our Lord! O our Lord!
O our Lord! O our Lord be our Ally.
Take care of us so that we are good.
In both universes, saved and truthful.
I'm standing outside Your door
hoping for your blessing.
I take refuge, I fled to You.
I entrust to You, preferably, my affairs.
O He who is the last resort and the final destination.
O the Benevolent, the All-Generous and the Protector.
I pour out to You the anguish of my heart,
and lead me to the good.
You have generosity and kindness
from which I hope to have benefited.
The purity of my soul and the goodness of my heart come from You,
make all my affairs wonderful.
Make good everything that appears or is hidden from me.
Make pure what is apparent or hidden from me.
Never come between You and me
an intermediary except the Prophet the ally.
Your loved one closer
Mohammad best of all people, the wonderful.
O our Lord pray over him,
on his family and his Companions the best prayer.
We seek in this prayer Your satisfaction
and Your approval which concretize our wishes and our solicitations.
