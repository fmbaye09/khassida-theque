O Allah there is no other deity but You.
O You who continuously bless me with favors.
Send prayers and peace O The All-Merciful 
upon him (the Prophet) through whom you make the times pleasant.
Our master Muhammad, his household 
and grant my request by his grace.
You are the All-Compassionate, The King, and The All-Holy.
I have to venerate and give thanks to You.
You are the Source of Peace, The Giver of tranquility, and The Bestower of security.
I invoke you through these attributes and say: Ameen.
You are the Almighty, The Lord, and The Restorer.
We owe You gratitude.
O You The Supremely Great, You only deserve to be proud.
O The Creator, The Evolver, and The Fashioner.
The All-Forgiving, The Ever- Dominant, and The Giver of gifts.
O You Giver of good news, and bad ones.
The Provider, O Revealer, and The Omniscient.
O You best of those who give asylum to the unjustly treated. 
The Withholder, The Expander, The Humbler.
The Exalter, O Source of lights.
The Bestower of Honor, The Disgracer, O The All-Hearing.
O You  The All-Seeing, The Responsive, best of those who are called. 
The Judge, The Equitable, The Subtle O The Knower of Realities.
The Forbearing, The Supreme indeed, You are the All-Forgiving.
The All-Appreciative, The Exalted O You The Greatest.
I owe You gratitude and glorification.
O Protector, O Sustainer, O The Accounter.
O The Glorious, O The Generous  O The Watchful.
O The Fulfiller of prayers, O The All-Embracing, O the Perfectly Wise.
O You who own the command and the decision.
You are the Most Affectionate, The Noble, and Resurrector.
By singing your praise you drive  forward all people.
You are The Witness, The Truth, and The Advocate.
I always place my trust in you.
You are The Inexhaustible, The Firm, and the Protecting friend.
To you, I always dedicate my alterations.
You are The praiseworthy Lord, You are The Reckoner.
I indeed cannot enumerate your praises.
O The Originator, The Renewer, Giver of Life, and the Life Taker.
You give life to the creation and cause them to die.
O the Ever-Living, The Self-Subsisting  The Resourceful.
O the Noble whom all slaves desire.
O the Unique, the Eternal, the Capable.
You are the owner and the Lord of mankind.
You are nicknamed by the All-Prevailing,
The Promoter, and the Delayer.
You are the Pre-Existing, the Ultimate, you are the Manifest 
You are the Hidden who knows everything.
You are also the Guardian, the Protecting friend 
You are The Mmost-High who repairs the affairs of all.
O You the Oft-Forgiving, the Gracious, and the Avenger.
I am satisfied with you then I never retaliate.
You are indeed the Forgiver and the Consoling.
The best  refuge for a terrorized.
O Master of Kingdom to whom 
I place all my trust Lord of Majesty and Generosity.
O You the Equitable, the Gatherer, the Self-Sufficient.
To you, I hope to obtain approval for my deeds.
You provide abundance to whomever.
You please in your creatures, O Enricher.
O you who comforted my heart 
and shielded me against harm, O Defender.
You who protect me against plagues and damage 
And from anything that I dislike, O the Distressor.
O You to whom by his grace 
the benefits are overflowing every time, O the Auspicious.
O You who always illuminate my heart 
because of his  perpetual supervision, O the Light.
To you, I turn with efforts 
hoping to obtain beyond my expectations, O the Guide.
O you to whom I owe gratefulness 
for having created everything in being, O Originator.
O You who included me in the circle of the honorable 
because of my positive opinion about Him, O the Ever-Lasting.
O You who sustains all farmers.
You are my treasure O the supreme Heir.
Guidance comes from you into my heart 
As well as purity O you who appoint to the right path.
Your Book (the Koran) which  contains the Psalms (of David) has purified me 
by Your grace O you the Most-Patient.
