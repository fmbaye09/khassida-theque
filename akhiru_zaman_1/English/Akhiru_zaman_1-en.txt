I thank the Lord possessor of happiness.
May peace and happiness be 
in His Prophet Muhammad as long as Islam lasts,
in his parents and his Companions who are the guides.
After that, here are the recommendations that are useful,
which are full of happiness and meaning.
That every believer must embody the divine orders
and recognize the Sunah of the Prophet who is the possessor of the truth.
This book is full of sense, meaning.
This book is filled with happiness.
It avoids the misdeeds of hell.
It leads to Heaven (which follows it).
This book allows all wishes and desires to be fulfilled.
It opens the ways of enlightenment to those who know it.
Because all that it contains as theory.
and what it contains as a guide (are unlimited).
I took it (this book) from the books of great scholars,
who are directed to the path of salvation.
It contains (this book) the recommendations of God
and the practices of the Prophet.
It allows the fulfillment of wishes,
to those who orient themselves towards God.
You must then comply with God's recommendation.
O! Muslim brothers, worship God and follow his orders.
After you have known and politely followed (his recommendations),
and all that takes away from the punishment of the Hereafter.
Follow the recommendations and practices of the Prophet,
and those of his venerated companions.
While being wary of the prohibitions of God,
and we ask you to beware of it, to follow the right path.
Do not seek equal to God, you friends,
for seeking Him an equal leads you to hell.
Kill your neighbor only to punish the voluntary clerk,
because this action is prohibited.
Don't approach out of selfishness
of adultery to be delighted.
Avoid speaking badly of someone who is absent,
beware of ostentation, of evil, of what banishes the bad.
Of the one who reports lies, vainess,
to use what is not ours, betrayal.
Do not look enviously at your neighbor.
Do not go with someone who neglects their religion unless you have to.
Repent as soon as possible if you have sinned.
Calm down when you are angry.
Conform to the right qualities.
Always beware of hypocrisy.
Be calm at all times.
Go to God.
Be ready for prayer,
as soon as the moment arrives.
Beware of the head and its surroundings,
belly and what it includes.
I mean by that the eyes (what they see), the ears (what they hear),
sex, tongue (what it says), legs (where they are).
Always ask for the blessing
as well as the greeting of our Prophet.
Hold on to wirds and implorations
of names of God nights and days.
Fast continuously three days each month,
month in which you felt well.
Repent constantly to God,
before death occurs in your life.
Welcome well the guest when he arrives to you.
Welcome whoever comes to you.
Visit a Muslim if he is sick.
Beware of prohibited games in life.
Beware of unnecessary chats,
orient yourself to the implorations of the names of God. Think about God earlier.
Pray at night, give food,
wish the good and consolidate your links.
Do not name the faults of others
and be brave.
Don't shoot yourself, don't run away.
Don't insult yourself, don't flatter yourself.
Don't quarrel, don't brag.
Do you want only good between yourselves, only do each other good.
Respect the marabouts as well as the elderly.
Have mercy on orphans as well as children.
Help the poor, the unhappy,
as well as the indebted.
