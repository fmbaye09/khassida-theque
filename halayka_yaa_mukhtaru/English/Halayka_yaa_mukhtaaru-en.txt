You must strive diligently
every hour on the path of righteousness.
And do not neglect piety and seek knowledge.
And do not smile at others, lest you regret it.
Be patient in adversity and hardship.
And remain calm and steadfast.
If you strive for knowledge while you are young,
you will achieve your goals when you are old.
And you will not miss out on the advantages, my brother
unless you lose patience with adversity.
Be patient and do not be lazy.
The lazy do not rule the world.
Avoid everything that the Almighty has forbidden.
Trust in what the Messenger has brought.
Do not associate with anyone who does not guide you.
And avoid any companion who destroys you.
If you are ignorant, become noble tomorrow
nn the company of the righteous, do not be swayed.
And a righteous person may become weak tomorrow.
Do not befriend the foolish, for they will lead you astray.
Put your trust in the Most Merciful
at all times, O young man of the future.
