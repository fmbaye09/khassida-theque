Whoever thought me to be other than the servant of the Chosen One,
I serve him with what he has chosen.
I serve him until I enter Paradise,
the abode of decision, mercy, and grace.
Whoever thought that he would be forever
satisfied with what my service seemed to be
Was misled by misguidance and deception
He obeys me in what is lawful.
I praised him in my speech before Libreville (Gabon).
Praise that surpasses that of all others.
I praised him with praise that surpasses that of Mayombe.
He erased my faults and forgave my sins.
I praised him before the people of Galwa.
Praise that was sufficient for me in times of hardship and affliction.
From me to him, service that has become necessary.
His garden has been magnified to the point of resurrection.
His grandson, Al-Jilani, is pleased with me.
Upon him is the satisfaction of the One who declared me.
His grandson Abu Al-Hasan is pleased with me
May he be pleased with Al-Hasan, who will live forever.
His grandson Al-Tijani is pleased with me.
May he be pleased with the destroyer of the jinn.
The four caliphs are pleased with me.
I have earned the satisfaction of those who are close and followed.
Whoever is not pleased with me, then so be it.
He is pleased with me, a pleasure that is not seen.
May God reward the Chosen One with the best reward
as He accepted and fulfilled His promise.
The prayers of those who honor him will meet me
on the one who threw me into the well.
